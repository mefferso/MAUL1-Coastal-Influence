# Phase 4 evidence design

Purpose: distinguish observational support for river, coastal and overlapping forcing at MAUL1, with an honest indeterminate outcome. No forecasts, probabilities, attribution fractions, alerts or PETSS changes.

Use the existing five-station archive and frozen revised Phase 3B event/control selections; do not modify Phase 3 outputs or acquisition. A standard-library pipeline writes a new phase4 directory. Rules are provisional physical screening hypotheses, frozen before evaluation, not independently verified labels.

At each completed UTC hour assess trailing observations only. Exclude flagged suspect hours, invalid values, insufficient coverage, units/datum mismatches and changes across datum boundaries. NWCL1 residual requires same-hour same-datum water/prediction. Completed hourly aggregates become replay eligible at hour end, not bin start. Original receipt times are unavailable.

River support: six consecutive hours of elevated DENL1 discharge or substantial persistent antecedent rise. Coastal support: six consecutive hours of positive NWCL1 residual and residual increase, independent MDLL1 change from a preceding baseline, and wind vector directed toward the western lake. All three coastal indicators must coexist. MCHL1 is supporting native-change context, never a required gate. Unknown differs from observed lack of support. No DENL1 signal cannot exclude local/tributary river inputs. Lake elevation cannot be compared across native datums.

Event interpretations examine the frozen onset through peak and station-specific antecedent windows (river 72h, coastal 18h); compound requires simultaneous pathway support. Disjoint signals are indeterminate with sequential context. Report first/last evidence times, coverage, missing and contradictory observations. For controls retain exact eligible seven-day windows; forcing evidence does not predict a MAUL1 rise. Event-level lookahead selection is explicitly retrospective.

Replay is a separate research dataset at fixed six-hour clock times within priority cases and event onset offsets (-24, 0, +12, +24h), plus control endpoints. Assessment never reads future event peaks or onset labels. Retrospective sampling times are catalog-conditioned; report this selection limitation.

Preserve fixed 2023 split/72h embargo, extend seam checks over actual feature baselines; do not tune thresholds from holdout. Calibrate only discharge percentiles from 2016–2022. Publish observed training distributions, frozen sensitivity variants, class transitions and per-subset counts without accuracy claims. Existing event recovery independence remains unchanged, and dependence of overlapping feature windows is audited separately.

Publish detailed four-case reports, machine-readable datasets, summary and sensitivity reports, compact website section and a reproducible no-download Actions job. Verify full archive outputs, deterministic rerun, tests, CI and deployment before completion.

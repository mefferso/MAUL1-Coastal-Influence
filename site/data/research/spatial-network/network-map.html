Geographic context
Lake Pontchartrain lies north of New Orleans. Lake Maurepas and the lower Amite approach lie west; Pass Manchac connects the two lakes. The Causeway crosses north-south through central lake. Dashed line and place labels are illustrative; no flow arrows or surveyed channel geometry are implied.
